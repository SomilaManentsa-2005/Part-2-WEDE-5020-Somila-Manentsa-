WHAT WE OFFER

Products & Export Services

African Foods Company (Ltd) markets and exports authentic South African nonperishable food products with a long shelf life to South Africans living overseas who struggle to find traditional items in local stores.

Our Premium Export Range

Biltong & Savoury Snacks

Vacuum-packed traditional South African biltong, drywors, and popular crunchy snacks like NikNaks, specially sealed and prepared for long shelf life during global distribution.

Teas & Soft Drinks

Exporting home-country favourites including organic Rooibos Tea, Stoney Ginger Beer, and Crème Soda to bring back familiar tastes to overseas homes.

Pantry Essentials & Sweets

Exporting glass-bottled and sealed staples like Mrs Ball's Chutney, Aromat, bottled atchaar, Ouma Rusks, and classic Chappies bubblegum directly across international borders.
