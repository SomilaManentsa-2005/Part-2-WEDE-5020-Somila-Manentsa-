About Our Enterprise

Our History

African Foods Company (Ltd) was founded to serve South Africans living abroad who struggle to find traditional foods in local overseas stores[cite: 1]. Operating from our Cape Town Head Office and Durban branch, we export quality nonperishable goods with long shelf lives[cite: 1].

Mission Statement

To connect South Africans around the globe with the authentic tastes of home through efficient global export operations, exceptional product preservation, and dependable multi-branch logistics[cite: 1].

Vision Statement

To become the leading international distributor of South African consumer products, recognized globally for superior supply chain security and unmatched customer service[cite: 1].
